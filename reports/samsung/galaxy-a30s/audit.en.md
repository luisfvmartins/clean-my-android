# Technical Audit — Samsung Galaxy A30s (SM-A307GT)

**Manufacturer:** Samsung Electronics  
**Commercial Model:** Galaxy A30s (`SM-A307GT`)  
**Platform / SoC:** Samsung Exynos 7904 (14nm Octa-core)  
**Operating System:** Android 11 · Samsung One UI 3.1  
**Homologation Level:** Level 3 (Subsystem functional validation completed; PDF printing limitation noted)

---

## 1. Executive Summary and Methodology

This technical audit documents the analysis and surgical debloat of the **Samsung Galaxy A30s** smartphone. Initial inspection via Android Debug Bridge (ADB) identified **392 packages cataloged** across factory system partitions (`/system`, `/product`, `/apex`, and `/data` stubs).

The goal was to neutralize intrusive commercial advertising packages (Digital Turbine Ignite), background diagnostic daemons, and unused vendor services while fully preserving telephony (RIL), biometrics, and One UI stability.

---

## 2. Initial Inventory and Analysis Batches

The full initial inventory is recorded in [`before/packages.txt`](before/packages.txt) and [`before/inventory.json`](before/inventory.json).

Interventions were conducted across two authorized batches:
* **Batch 1 (Commercial Agents and Advertising):** Deactivation of *Digital Turbine Ignite* (`com.dti.samsung`), Meta/Facebook telemetry services (`com.facebook.katana`, `com.facebook.system`, `com.facebook.appmanager`, `com.facebook.services`), and Game Optimizing Service (`com.samsung.android.game.gos`).
* **Batch 2 (Diagnostics and Unused Vendor Features):** Deactivation of analytic daemons including *DiagMonAgent* (`com.sec.android.diagmonagent`), *IMS Logger* (`com.sec.imslogger`), and partner stubs.

The complete list of 46 user-level disabled packages and 2 user-level uninstalled packages is available in:
* [`changes/disabled-packages.txt`](changes/disabled-packages.txt)
* [`changes/removed-packages.txt`](changes/removed-packages.txt)

---

## 3. Risk Assessment and Protected Components

No critical telephony components (`com.android.phone`, `com.android.server.telecom`), biometric authentication services, or essential user interface components were modified.

As documented in [`changes/appops-changes.txt`](changes/appops-changes.txt) and [`docs/MANUAL_CHANGES.md`](../../../docs/MANUAL_CHANGES.md), no AppOps or manifest permissions were modified in isolation, preventing side-effects on shared UIDs.

---

## 4. Limitations and Identified Regression

* **Printing / Save as PDF:** Disabling `com.android.printspooler` caused the loss of the native "Save as PDF" capability in the system viewer. This limitation was accepted by the owner and motivated permanently protecting this component in the project's global safeguard list (`data/protected_essential_packages.txt`).
* **Residual Telemetry:** Disabling specific daemons stops traffic generated by those components, but does not prove the complete elimination of all Google, carrier, or Samsung telemetry at kernel or core service levels.
