# Motorola Moto G54 5G — Audit Summary

**Device:** Motorola Moto G54 5G · XT2343-1 · `cancunf`  
**System:** Android 15 · Hello UI  
**Publication status:** Technical inventory published  
**Script compatibility:** Not approved by this report

## Scope

The public audit documents a device-specific inventory of Android system and manufacturer components. The local collection contains 462 components. The public version intentionally omits 29 user-installed applications and documents 394 system/manufacturer components.

The detailed inventory is split into seven batch files under [docs/auditorias/moto-g54/](auditorias/moto-g54/). The English index is available at [docs/auditorias/moto-g54/README.en.md](auditorias/moto-g54/README.en.md); the detailed batch files and original full report remain in Portuguese.

## Important limitations

- The published inventory is a record of one specific device and software configuration.
- It does not mean every component was individually functionally tested.
- Package classifications are not authorization to disable or uninstall packages.
- No conclusion should be generalized to another model, region, or firmware version.
- The report does not approve the repository's debloating scripts for this device.
- User-installed applications and raw dumps must remain private.
